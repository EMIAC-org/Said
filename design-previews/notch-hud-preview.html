The minimal listening UX

Idle: bare notch. Hotkey → an amber recording dot appears in the notch bar (left of the camera) and the chin expands along the x-axis, growing wider as you speak.

The chin shows only the live transcript — no "LISTENING" label, no timer, no audio bars. Dot colour tracks the phase: amber listening → periwinkle polishing → green pasted.

The "blackhead" rule

Notched Mac: dot sits in the bar beside the real camera; transcript drops below the camera line; chin flares into the bezel. Flat display: synthetic floating pill, same behaviour. Toggle above to compare.
