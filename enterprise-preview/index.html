Why Said Enterprise

Your VPS, Your Data

Deploy on your own infrastructure. Meeting transcripts, summaries, and tasks never leave your servers. Full data sovereignty.

Zero Speaker Diarization

Each person's device captures only their voice. No ML guesswork about who said what — speaker identity is solved at the hardware level.

Lark-Native

OAuth login, contact picker, task sync, doc export — all through Lark's Open API. No new accounts, no separate workspace.

How It Works
1

Connect

Employees sign in via Lark OAuth. Said desktop links to their identity — name, avatar, department. One-time setup.

2

Meet

Admin creates a meeting on the dashboard, picks participants from Lark contacts. Everyone sees it in their Said app and joins.

3

Capture

Each device records its owner's voice, streams through Local speech STT, and pushes polished transcript chunks to the meeting server via WebSocket.

4

Synthesize

The server's AI watches the live transcript — builds a rolling summary, extracts action items, detects task assignments, flags decisions.

5

Sync

Meeting ends. One click pushes tasks to Lark Tasks (assigned per person), summary to a Lark Doc, full transcript as attachment.
