Before your first meeting
1

Open Meetings. If the local model banner appears, download the recommended model once.

2

On macOS, allow Screen Recording so AirNote can hear system audio from Zoom, Meet, Teams, and other meeting apps.

3

Reopen AirNote after granting the permission. The + button becomes available when setup is ready.

Your recording, transcript, and meeting files stay on this device.
02

Start—there is no waiting room

AirNote does not need a meeting link. Start your Zoom, Meet, Teams, or in-person call normally, then click the plus button in AirNote.

Start recording
1

Click + on the Meetings page.

2

The Live Meeting screen opens immediately. Wait a moment while the capture status changes from starting to recording.

3

Return to your meeting app. AirNote keeps capturing until you explicitly end the meeting.

You can leave the AirNote window in the background. Do not quit AirNote while the meeting is running.

05

End when the conversation is over

The red phone control stops the local recording. AirNote asks for confirmation so an accidental click does not end the capture.

End this meeting?

Recording will stop and the meeting will be processed into a summary.

Finish safely
1

Click the red Leave meeting control.

2

Choose Keep recording if the meeting is still running.

3

Choose End meeting to stop capture and begin transcription, cleanup, and summary generation.

06

AirNote wraps up in the background

After the brief handoff screen, AirNote opens the recorded meeting and shows each processing stage above the detail tabs.

After recording
1

You may briefly see Wrapping up your meeting… while AirNote closes the recording.

2

The processing banner advances through Recording → Transcribing → Cleaning → Summarizing.

3

You may navigate to another AirNote screen while this runs. Keep AirNote open until processing completes.

If summary generation fails, the transcript is still saved and the banner offers a retry.
